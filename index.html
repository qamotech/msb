🦎 Ms. Frizzle's Kickoff Script

"Seatbelts, everyone! Please let this be a normal field trip... With the Friz? No way! Today, we are taking a shocking journey into the invisible world of electrons! We're going to build glowing circuits, test mystery materials, and even create our own magnets out of thin air. So, take chances, make mistakes, and get messy!"

🔬
4 Hands-On Stations (Materials for 10 Setups Each) Before students begin: Set up all 4 stations and divide materials into 10 student setups. Use the circuit from Lab 1 in Lab 2 for the conductor test. Adult supervision required at the electromagnet station.

Lab 1: Simple Circuits ("Gets Charged")

Station 1

Objective: Students create a complete closed circuit loop to light a miniature bulb.

Material Qty (10x) Notes
Batteries (AA / D) 10 1 per setup
Miniature light bulbs & sockets 10 Standard 1.5V-3V
Alligator clip wire pairs 10 pairs Red & black
Steps: Clip wire to positive battery terminal -> attach other end to bulb socket -> connect second wire from socket back to negative terminal. Watch the bulb glow!

Lab 2: Conductors vs. Insulators

Station 2

Objective: Test mystery materials in the circuit gap to classify conductors vs. insulators.

Test Item Conductor? Observation
Copper Penny / Metal Fork ✅ Yes Bulb lights brightly
Plastic Spoon / Rubber Band ❌ No Bulb remains off
Aluminum Foil / Paperclip ✅ Yes Completes loop
Wooden Craft Stick ❌ No Insulator blocks flow
Steps: Unclip one wire from battery. Insert test item into the gap. If the bulb lights, electrons are flowing (conductor); if not, it's an insulator.

Lab 3: Build an Electromagnet

Station 3

Objective: Transform an iron nail into an active temporary magnet using electric current.

Material Qty (10x) Notes
Large Iron Nails (3-4 in) 10 Magnetic core
Insulated Copper Magnet Wire 10 spools 30-50 tight coils
D-Cell Heavy Duty Batteries 10 High current
Steel Paperclips Box 100+ Pickup test
⚠️ Caution: The wire and nail become warm after continuous contact. Disconnect from battery terminals between test runs!

Lab 4: Static Electricity & Sparks

Station 4

Objective: Transfer electrons via mechanical friction and observe electrostatic forces.

Material Qty (10x) Experiment
Latex Balloons 20 Inflate & charge
Wool Cloths / Felt Squares 10 Rub for 30 seconds
Tissue Paper Confetti Dots 10 cups Leaping paper dots
Empty Aluminum Soda Cans 10 Rolling can race
Steps: Rub balloon vigorously with wool cloth for 30s. Hold near empty soda can to roll it without touching, and hover over tissue confetti to watch dots dance!

📖 Class 1 Core Science Vocabulary Flashcards

Master terms from CMSB Class 1 DOCS.txt with interactive flashcards.

⚡ STATIC#01

An electric charge that gathers on the surface of an object through friction rather than flowing continuously as a current.

💡 ELECTRICITY#02

The flow of electrical energy through a closed circuit carried by tiny moving charges called electrons.

🧤 FRICTION#03

The physical resistance that occurs when two surfaces rub against each other, transferring electrons and producing thermal energy.

⚛️ ELECTRONS#04

Subatomic particles with negative charge that orbit atomic nuclei and flow through conductive metals to power devices.

🔥 HEAT#05

A form of energy transferred between materials due to temperature differences, electrical resistance, or mechanical friction.

🔄 TRANSFER#06

The relocation or transmission of energy, charge, or particles from one material or body to another.
