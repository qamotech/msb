HOW TO SHIFT

Your mech runs by itself. Shift into the right form before each hazard:

🏎️RACER1A←

Slides under lasers. Hits boost pads. Action: Nitro.

✈️JET2S↑

Flies over pits. Action: Blasters vs drones.

🤖TITAN3D→

Smashes walls. Action: Shield vs beams.

⚡ Action: SPACE or the big button. Shift just before a hazard for a PERFECT and build your combo.

👁 The Warden boss telegraphs its beams: ground beams need the Jet, sky beams need a ground form.

🌈 PERFECTs and Morph Gates fill OVERDRIVE: 6 seconds of smashing through everything at triple score.

🚪 Morph Gates show a form icon. Pass through in that form for a bonus. 🔨 Crushers slam on a rhythm: go when the light is green.

👆 Touch: tap the form buttons, or swipe up (Jet), down (Titan), sideways (Racer); tap to act. Keys Q/E cycle forms.
