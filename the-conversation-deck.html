A simple framework

Good posture.
Every time.

You don't need to sound impressive. You need to be present, genuinely curious, and comfortable letting the conversation unfold.

👂

Listen before leading

Follow the part of their answer that has energy. One thoughtful follow-up beats three questions in a row.

🤝

Make it mutual

Share a little of your own experience, too. A conversation is an exchange, not an interview.

🕊️

Leave room for no

People can be content where they are. Respect their answer instead of treating it like an obstacle.

A little reminder

Less strategy.
More presence.

🌉

Start with a bridge

Connect the question to something real: a recent post, a shared experience, or something they mentioned. Relevance makes curiosity feel natural.

🔎

Follow their words

If they say they're building, learning, or exploring something, ask what drew them to it. Let their answer shape the next question.

🗣️

Share your side

Offer a short, authentic example from your own life. Then give them room to respond without immediately moving to another question.

🌿

Let the answer be enough

A short answer isn't an invitation to push harder. If the conversation isn't opening up, respect the moment and let it breathe.
